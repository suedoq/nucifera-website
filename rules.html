Rules

Play fair. Stay welcome.

Nucifera has some rules you must follow. There are only five of them, so please read them all.

Zero tolerance

If you break any of these rules you will get permanently banned.

Game rules

  1. No hacking and macros

    Cheats like X-ray and KillAura, bots like Baritone, and minimaps which let you see caves are not allowed.

    Not allowed

    • X-ray, Freecam, ESP or anything that lets you see what normal vanilla players can't
    • Baritone or any automation scripts
    • Macros and inventory management mods
    • Any modification that gives you an advantage in combat: aim assist, kill aura, auto totem etc.

    Allowed

    • Fullbright
    • Dumb auto-clickers
    • Vanilla F3 pie chart
    • Minimaps without a cave view feature
    • Waypoint mods

  2. No mass terrain destruction

    Do not make lavacasts or blow up big areas.

Chat rules

  1. No hate speech or discrimination

    Occasional swearing is fine, just don't overstep the line.

  2. No self-promotion or advertisements

    Do not advertise anything.

  3. No political or religious topics

    You are free to believe what you want, just don't talk about your beliefs on this server.

Not sure if a mod is allowed?

Ask on the Nucifera Discord before you use it.
